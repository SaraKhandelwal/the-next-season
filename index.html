THE KEY FINDING

Most of the visible jump came after the moment.

March 2024 recorded 18,664 Fever article views. April recorded 146,680, the month of the April 15 draft. A monthly attention series alone offered little warning of the magnitude of the rise.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

Attention is not a purchase order.

The team-store growth figures are published cumulative year-over-year results. They cannot be reconciled to Wikipedia traffic or broken into product categories, prices, channels or margins from this public data.
